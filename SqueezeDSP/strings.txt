	EN	How our own decoder turns a DSF file into PCM for the DSP. PLUGIN_SQUEEZEDSP_SRC
	EN	Sample Rate Converter

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEDSP_SRC_HELP
	EN	Off: every track goes out as it is, PCM at its own rate and a DSD file as DSD Processing's Output says. PCM: every track, DSD included, goes to the player's top rate, in its own family (Per family) or one rate for all (Player max), up to the Max rate. DSD: every track, PCM included, goes out as DSD at the DSD rate you choose (the highest the player and its DAC take), through the DSP at DSD Processing's DSP rate and our own modulator; only for a player that takes native DSD itself (it lists dsf). PCM and DSD each have their own filters, shown with their options: one for a 44.1 or 48 kHz track, one for hi-res, the engine resampling in 64-bit floating point. Each is a filter, its length and its phase. Kaiser and Gaussian remove every image above the track's band; Half-band, Slow roll-off and Non-ringing let some through, for listening (going down in rate they run as Kaiser short). Phase: linear rings evenly before and after a transient; minimum has no ringing before it; intermediate is in between; Intermediate 80% and 70% sit nearer linear, the top of the band less delayed. Length: short rings for the least time; long keeps more of the top of the band flat.
