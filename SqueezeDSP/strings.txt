PLUGIN_SQUEEZEDSP_UPSAMPLE
	EN	Upsample

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEDSP_UPSAMPLE_HELP
	EN	Resample every track before the DSP, with the SoX resampler (soxr) in ffmpeg, in floating point. Target rate: Per family sends each track to the highest rate in its own family (44.1 or 48 kHz) that this player takes; Player max sends every track, DSD included, to the highest rate the player reports, so it never changes rate between tracks (the other family's tracks then take a non-integer ratio, which costs a little more CPU). A track already at its target rate goes to the DSP as it is, without the resampler. A DSF file is decoded by our own DSD decoder straight to the target (see DSD); a DFF file gets there through the same soxr and options, always at linear phase: ffmpeg's soxr has no other. Inter-sample peaks then reach the DSP as real samples: the log summary's peaks show them, and Preamp gives them headroom. Phase: linear rings evenly before and after a transient; minimum has no ringing before it; intermediate is in between. Intermediate and minimum use SoX's own resampler, which works in fixed point, so 6 dB is taken off before it and put back after: the level is the same, and only an inter-sample peak above +6 dBTP is clipped. Precision: the filter's accuracy in bits. Passband: where the filter starts to roll off, as a share of the track's Nyquist frequency: 99% by default per family; with Player max 95% by default and at most 95% (a higher choice plays at 95%); Auto lets soxr choose its own edge (flat to about 92%). Steep: flat right up to the passband edge (a Chebyshev passband). The DSP then runs at the higher rate and uses more CPU.

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEDSP_DSD
	EN	DSD

PLUGIN_SQUEEZEDSP_DSD_HELP
	EN	How our own decoder turns a DSF file into PCM for the DSP. Low-pass: DSD carries strong noise above the audio band; the filter removes it before the DSP and the DAC see it (30 kHz recommended, 24 kHz conservative, 50 kHz permissive; None passes it on). Gain: DSD plays 6 dB below PCM, and +6 dB brings it level; with the low-pass on, its peaks are the music and not the noise. A DFF file is decoded by ffmpeg as before.
